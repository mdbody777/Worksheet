  16. Acceptable range: A biological vector requires the parasite to undergo essential development or multiplication inside it, while a paratenic host acts only as a refuge or transport vehicle where no development occurs. (1 mark)
  17. Acceptable range: A facultative parasite can survive either as a free-living organism in the environment or as a parasite depending on the conditions, whereas an obligate parasite will die without a host. (1 mark)
  18. Acceptable range: The degree to which a parasite is restricted to one or a few closely related host species to successfully establish and complete its life cycle. (1 mark)
  19. Acceptable range: It often migrates abnormally (visceral larva migrans) or causes severe reactions, and it fails to complete its life cycle because the new host's internal environment does not provide the specific cues needed for its development. (1 mark)
